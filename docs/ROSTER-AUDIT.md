# Playable roster audit

Canonical source: [PokeAPI species and evolution relationships](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/blob/master/data/v2/csv/pokemon_species.csv), retrieved 2026-10-25. The bundled snapshot includes all species so later-generation evolutions are considered, even when the evolved species is not in this game. See POKEAPI-LICENSE.md.

Before: **220 base species**. After: **137 selectable base species**. Removed from selection: **83**. All 17 existing Mega forms remain battle forms of eligible bases. Source species, sprites, shared moves, historical records and cinematic artwork remain archived in place.

Kanto Farfetch’d has no evolution; only Galarian Farfetch’d evolves into Sirfetch’d. Kanto Mr. Mime is final; only Galarian Mr. Mime evolves into Mr. Rime. The two regional-only edges are explicitly excluded for the Kanto forms shipped here. Mime Jr. being a baby does not disqualify final-stage Mr. Mime. Scyther, Primeape, Chansey, Magneton and similar species are excluded because later-generation evolutions exist. No unresolved form ambiguity remains in the current roster. Unshipped regional forms are not implicitly admitted.

Saved teams are backed up once to msb.teams.pre-final-v1. Existing eligible members and Mega choices remain; removed members prefer available final descendants, then eligible type-related replacements. Duplicates/unknowns are repaired to the saved team size. Re-reading is idempotent. Mastery for removed species stays under its original ID and is never reassigned. Old story/leader battle pools resolve to eligible forms; narrative actors can still show earlier stages.
