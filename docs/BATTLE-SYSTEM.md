# Battle system overhaul — audit, architecture and phases

Connected moves resolve without accuracy or evasion rolls. Range, tracking, dodges and protections still
determine whether a hit lands. Accuracy-only moves, one-hit KO moves and Bright Powder are no longer available in battle kits.

## Audit (10 Nov 2026)

How it works today:
- **Moves** (`src/moves.js`): one big object of hand-written specs. Each spec mixes mechanics (type, power,
  cooldown) with presentation (an `Action` class plus its parameters: `Melee`, `Dash`, `Shot`, `Shockwave`,
  `Stream`, `BeamAttack`, `Utility`, custom classes). About 140 regular moves; most Pokémon draw from them.
  Rayquaza, Lugia and the other legendaries have custom Action classes and ultimates (Dragon Ascent,
  Aeroblast…) with multi-stage choreography: a windup with energy gathering, a telegraph, movement of the
  user itself, a release, ground marks, a tuned impact and camera feedback. That staging is what makes them
  better — not the particles alone.
- **Damage** (`src/combat.js` `applyHit`): power × attack/defence × type × STAB × weather × combo × crit.
  Immunities are softened to 0.5× by design ("every move stays usable"); weather is ×1.3 / ×0.7.
- **Stats**: atk/def/spa/spdef/speed per species; changes are timed multipliers (`buffs`), not stages.
  Speed only scales walking speed.
- **Abilities** (`src/data/abilities*.js`): hook objects; many adapted loosely (Chlorophyll is ×1.35 walk speed).
- **Weather**: ability weather lasts while the setter is on the field; moves set 20s weather. Solar Beam
  ignores weather. No accuracy exists, so Thunder/Hurricane/Blizzard can't interact with weather.
- **Movesets**: the 390 new species got four generated attacks; no status, setup, recovery or signature moves.
- **Placeholders**: Castform's Forecast changes type only (no form sprite); Leech Seed, Toxic, Protect,
  Thunder Wave, sleep moves, Dragon Dance, Calm Mind, pivoting and most status moves don't exist.

## Architecture (target)

| Piece | File | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Move database | `src/data/movedex.js` (generated by `scripts/build-movedex.py` from Showdown's move table) | type, category, power, accuracy, priority, flags, secondary effects, boosts, drain/recoil, heal, weather/terrain/hazard, volatile effects |
| Movesets | `src/data/movesets.js` (generated from Smogon Random Battle sets, gen 9 → 8 → 7) | four species-appropriate moves, chosen ability, item and Tera type per Pokémon |
| Animation recipes | `src/data/move-anims.js` | per move: delivery (melee, dash, projectile, beam, stream, wave, rain, pillar, self) + stages (charge, release, travel, impact) with element palette, shape, intensity and timing |
| Move builder | `src/moves.js` (`buildMove`) | combines movedex + recipe into the engine's move spec; hand-made legendary moves keep their custom Actions |
| Effect resolver | `src/effects-resolver.js` | runs a move's effects at the right moment: before (priority, protect checks), on hit (damage, secondary), after (stat stages, heal, drain, recoil, pivot, field) |
| Stats | `src/stats.js` | stat stages (−6…+6, the games' multipliers) and effective Speed (stages, abilities, items, paralysis) used by movement *and* move recharge |
| Weather | `src/game.js` + `combat.js` | timed weather (abilities too), the games' modifiers, weather-dependent moves and abilities, residual damage |
| Battle events | `game.emit(type, payload)` | mechanics, animation, log and HUD subscribe to the same events (ability triggered, form change, stat change, weather start/end, protect, miss…) |
| Validation | `tests/battle-rules.test.js` + `scripts/validate-moves.mjs` | acceptance scenarios and a check that every move in every moveset has mechanics and an animation recipe |

## Phase 2 design (move database + movesets)

1. `scripts/build-movedex.py <cache dir>` reads Showdown's `moves.json` and the Smogon Random Battle sets
   (gen 9 → 8 → 7, cached in the scratch `dex` folder; re-download from play.pokemonshowdown.com/data/moves.json
   and pkmn.github.io/randbats/data/gen{9,8,7}randombattle.json) and writes:
   - `src/data/movedex.js`: every move any roster Pokémon's set uses, as `{ name, type, cat, power, acc, pri, flags,
     secondary, self, boosts, status, volatile, heal, drain, recoil, multihit, weather, terrain, hazard, selfSwitch }`.
   - `src/data/smogon.js`: `SMOGON_MOVES[species] = [4 moves]` chosen from its Smogon role (signature and STAB
     attacks first, then the role's setup/recovery/status, then coverage), alongside `SMOGON_ABILITIES`.
2. `src/moves.js` `buildMove(id)`: any movedex move without a hand-made spec becomes an engine move: physical
   contact → `Melee` (priority / "rush" moves → `Dash`), special → `Shot`, beam-named moves → `BeamAttack`,
   spread moves (Earthquake, Surf, Discharge…) → `Shockwave`, status → `Utility` with resolver effects; colours from
   the type palette, shape and timing from `src/data/move-anims.js` (per-move overrides). Hand-made moves win.
3. Effect resolver (`src/effects-resolver.js`), called from `applyHit` (secondary: status chance, flinch, target
   stat drops, self drops like Close Combat / Draco Meteor, drain, recoil, multihit) and from `Utility` (status
   moves: boosts, status, heal, Rest, Wish, weather, terrain, hazards, Defog, Haze, Substitute, Taunt, Trick,
   Protect family, pivot).
4. Species use `SMOGON_MOVES` (four moves), keeping their ultimate; Megas inherit. Validation
   (`scripts/validate-moves.mjs`): every moveset move exists in MOVES with mechanics and an animation recipe.
